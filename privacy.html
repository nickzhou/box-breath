Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Box Breath does not collect, store, or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your chosen voice, sound, time, and pace are saved in your browser's local storage so the app remembers them. This information never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics inside the app.
  • No microphone, camera, location, or contacts access.

Voices and sounds

Spoken guidance uses the text-to-speech voices built into your device. Nature sounds are generated on your device. Nothing is recorded.

Hosting

Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Children

Box Breath is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Health notice

Box Breath is a relaxation aid, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. Stop if you feel dizzy. Ask your doctor if you have a health condition.
