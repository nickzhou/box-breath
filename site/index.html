How box breathing works

  1. 4Breathe in
  2. 4Hold
  3. 4Breathe out
  4. 4Hold

Four equal sides, like a box. Repeat for a few minutes. The technique was made popular by former Navy SEAL commander Mark Divine, who taught it to stay calm and focused under pressure.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, no subscription, and no advertising.

How do I put it on my home screen?

On iPhone or iPad, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen." On Android, open the browser menu and tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen." It then opens like any other app and works offline.

Which voices do I get?

Box Breath uses the voices already built into your device, so the list varies. Most phones include several English accents in male and female voices.

Is it safe for children and older adults?

Box breathing is gentle. Choose the "Gentle" pace to start. Stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable. If you have a heart or lung condition or are pregnant, ask your doctor first. This is not medical advice.

Do you collect my data?

No. Your settings stay on your device. See the privacy policy.
